{{example:random_arrays}}

`randomArray`, `randomArray2d` and `randomArrayComplex` draw uniform values in `[0, 1)`. For normally distributed samples with a fixed seed, use `NormalDistribution.sample` from the [Normal distribution](/docs/normal-distribution) guide.
