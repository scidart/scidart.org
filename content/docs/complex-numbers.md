`Complex` holds a `real` and an `imaginary` part and supports `+`, `-`, `*` and `/`. Helper functions cover the rest: `complexAbs`, `complexConjugate`, `complexExp`, `complexCos`, `complexMultiplyScalar`, `complexDivideScalar`.

{{example:complex_numbers}}

Arrays of complex values are `ArrayComplex`, with element-wise versions such as `arrayComplexAbs` and `arrayComplexConjugate`. `arrayToComplexArray` converts a real `Array`.
